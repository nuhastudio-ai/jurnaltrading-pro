Manajemen Pair Trading
Cara Hitung PnL berdasarkan Tipe:
🔸 Forex Major (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, dll): PnL = (Exit−Entry) × Lot × 100.000 (pip 0.0001)
🔸 Commodity ×100 (XAU/USD Gold): PnL = (Exit−Entry) × Lot × 100 (pip 0.01 = $1/lot mini)
🔸 Commodity ×1000 (Oil, XAG): PnL = (Exit−Entry) × Lot × 1.000
🔸 JPY Pairs (USD/JPY dll): PnL = (Exit−Entry) × Lot × 1.000 (pip 0.01)
🔸 Custom: masukkan multiplier & pip size sendiri
